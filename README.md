A code-first research program on the entanglement structure of the quantum vacuum.

## What is new here, and what is not

found fewer new things than the layer table said. Stated once, in the table's own terms:

  QEI infimum by operator-space TEBD (no exact infimum for an interacting lattice theory was
  found in the literature, re-checked 2026-09-23 against its compared authors' later papers; its numbers are diagnosed — a first-order quartic tax, resolved at
  λ ≤ 1, bracketed at λ ≥ 2); the Gaussian-sampled 4d QEI constant, `C_4d/C_FE = 0.4574904`
  (the sharp 4d bound has no known closed form; re-checked 2026-09-23 against the whole
  Fewster–Ford–Roman / Schiappacasse–Fewster–Ford series and the 252 papers citing it or the two
  foundational QEI references, no Gaussian-sampled 4d value was found — the one source known to hold 4d sharp computations,
  Dawson's 2006 York thesis, is not online; record in `docs/LITERATURE_CHECK_2026-09-23.md`); the mutual-information tomography floor
  (a methods point that transfers); and the device NO-GO of `circuit-qed-noise-thresholds`,
  conditional on the circuit mapping. **Added 2026-09-12:** the **lower** half of the 4d
  Gaussian constant is now a computer-assisted theorem, `C_4d(Gaussian)/C_FE ≥ 0.457490709365`,
  from an exhibited 64-mode state verified by interval arithmetic with no quadrature in ω;
  with Fewster–Eveson's cited `R ≤ 1` as the upper half, the certified enclosure is
  `[0.4574907, 1]`, one-sided in substance and not to be quoted as a narrow one.
- **Not new, though this file said it was (corrected 2026-09-23):** the 4d Lorentzian-weight
  QEI infimum, 2.53× Fewster–Ford–Roman's 2012 moment estimate, listed here as a finding
  against a published number and as the program's one outward-moving wall. Schiappacasse, Fewster & Ford, PRD 97, 025013 (2018)
  — two of the three 2012 authors — published it (`C_shift = −0.0593338` for the same operator
  and weight), and this program's `0.059762` reproduces it to 0.72 %. What may still be this
  program's own is small: the 2012 number reproduced by the 2012 method, and a demonstration
  of why that method missed by a factor of 2.5. Nor is the diagonalization route new: Dawson's
  2006 York thesis and the 2018 paper both compute the sharp bound as the lowest eigenvalue of
  the diagonalized smeared operator.
- **Also known, found by the second round of that re-check (`docs/LITERATURE_CHECK_2026-09-23.md`):**
  the single-qubit extraction formula of the energy-teleportation resource theory is Salvia, De
  Palma & Giovannetti's local-ergotropy closed form (PRA 107, 012405 (2023)); harvest-then-teleport's
  "correlation, not negativity, pays" and its mutual-information bound are Perarnau-Llobet et al.
  (PRX 2015) and Manzano et al. (PRL 2018) in a new setting; the geometry failure map's
  off-criticality half is Leighton-Trudel (arXiv:2507.09749, 2025); and energy teleportation had
  run on IBM hardware before this program's single ibm_fez job (Ikeda, Phys. Rev. Applied 2023;
  Xie et al. and Hassan et al., 2024). What survives in each is listed in `docs/PRIORITY.md`.
- **Proved, but weaker than the conjecture it was aimed at (2026-09-12):** Layer 5's multimode
  amplification bound for **non-commuting** modulation patterns. Theorem M
  (`ln max|eig S_F|/T ≤ (d/2)‖K₀^{−1/4}PK₀^{−1/4}‖ ≤ ω_max d_eff/2`, every measurable |s| ≤ 1) is
  proved and near-tight in its operator-norm form; the conjecture with the single-mode constant
  λ*(d_eff) is **neither proved nor refuted**: the attack **reached** it — the largest archived
  ratio is 1.000000000000017 (refined 1.000000000000036), so the bound was attained and exceeded
  by +1.7e−14, inside the 10⁻¹³ roundoff of ln ρ, at an effectively single-mode configuration
  (a detuned pair driven to ω → (1, 1)) where Theorem B already attains it — and never broke it
  (`conjecture_refuted = false`). The proof technique is standard enough that Theorem M is held
  POSSIBLY KNOWN; a search on 2026-09-23 did not find it as stated. Theorem F, the two-mode
  sum-frequency rate, is classical combination-resonance theory.
- **Measured rather than assumed, in a toy that previously only checked (2026-09-12):** in 2+1
  the toy-Jacobson first law **measures** the free scalar's conformal-improvement coefficient,
  ξ = 0.1278 ± 0.0040 against (d−2)/(4(d−1)) = 1/8, identifying its shape as well as its value.
  The coefficient is textbook; that entanglement equilibrium is sharp enough to measure it is
  the new part, and the improvement's entangling-surface contact term is dropped, not derived. Two precedents
  found 2026-09-23: Javerzat & Tonni (JHEP 2022) confirmed the sphere's lattice entanglement
  Hamiltonian against the conformal prediction in two and three spatial dimensions, and Herzog &
  Nishioka (JHEP 2016) derived the boundary term this toy drops.
- **Reproductions at certified precision, previously labelled discoveries:** the n ≥ 3 interval
  result is a lattice check of Casini–Huerta's own n-interval formula (CQG 26, 185005 (2009)),
  screening included; the gapped triangle with s(m) = (2/π) k I(k′) is Eisler's exact boost
  operator for this very chain (J. Stat. Mech. (2025) 013101). Confirming them on the lattice
  with clean 1/L² convergence is useful; calling them discoveries was wrong.
- **Possibly known:** the artanh(d) bound and the rate optimum of Layer 5 — bang-bang optimality
  of parametric gain is folklore in the Meissner–Hill and swing-control literature; the per-zero
  statement for measurable drives and the closed-form λ*(d) were not found, and are held as a
  rederivation with citations until a control theorist has read `vacuum/floquet/bounds.py`.
- **Re-examined and de-circularized:** toy Jacobson (L2) — with the weight free, the wave chain's
  minimizer is the CHM parabola (overlap 1.0000) and Lifshitz is distinguished by the complexity of the admissible local weight, not its existence (CHM is the 3-mode parabola for the wave chain; Lifshitz needs ≥ 11–12 modes of a bumpy profile — 0.09 at 8 modes; below 1e−2 by 11–13 modes and below 1e−3 by 12–14, z-dependent; 5e−4 only by 20 Chebyshev modes — so the toy tests parsimony); the "recovers v² = 0.980" claim is withdrawn,
  velocity being a unit under a free weight. **Diagnosed:** the interacting λφ⁴ numbers (L5) — the
  residual beyond any quadratic reference is a first-order quartic tax (dR/dλ = +0.081), resolved at
  λ ≤ 1 (1.4 / 1.5 / 2.6 %) and **bracketed, not resolved, at λ ≥ 2**; **twelve** earlier statements are retracted, listed
  once in that candidate's §6.7 — the **eighth on 2026-09-12**, an operator-truncation bound at
  λ = 4 that the candidate's own exact-diagonalisation anchor refuted (the deviation at the sweep's
  own Fock cutoff is +39.4 %, and it *grows* with the cutoff). **The λ ≥ 2 magnitude is not closed**, and more of the same
  convergence plane will not close it; no such number is quoted anywhere as a *value*, only as a

  at λ = 2 and [−34.349 %, +15.131 %] at λ = 4. All 36 rows landed on 2026-09-13 and the

  on the same day, elsewhere:** toy Jacobson's claim that projecting the soft even modes makes the

  invariant; per unit variation it reverses), and the hardware candidate's "minimal depth 8" for the

Where the value is, in the reviewer's words: the infrastructure — a certified Gaussian and
lattice-QFT toolkit with the literature as regression tests, executable audits and a precision
ladder resolving margins to 1e−30 — and reproduction at certified precision in a field with no
replication culture. `docs/PRIORITY.md` has one row per candidate.


**Governing principle.** The vacuum is *closed* as an energy source and *open* as a
structured, correlated, manipulable medium. The closures — passivity of the ground state,
quantum energy inequalities, causality — are encoded as executable invariants (the
standing audits) and are never contested by the program. The openings — correlation
harvesting, energy redistribution and teleportation, amplification, geometry from
entanglement, vacuum design — are the search space. The free-field vacuum is a Gaussian
state: on a lattice it is fully specified by a covariance matrix, and every observable of
interest is exact linear algebra on that matrix. That is why code can carry this.

The full plan (Layers 0–7, anchors, pivot triggers, bibliography) is **[PLAN.md](PLAN.md)**.
The binding coordination contract (conventions, signatures) is **[docs/API.md](docs/API.md)**.
- **No wall moved outward.** An earlier version of this README said one had: the 4d Lorentzian-sampled QEI infimum, an exhibited state permitting 2.53× more negative energy than the 2012 literature estimate. That value was published in 2018 by two of the 2012 authors (Schiappacasse, Fewster & Ford, PRD 97, 025013 (2018)), and this program reproduces it. Every result here revises the interior of a region the literature had already fixed.

## Install and run

```bash
python3 -m venv .venv
.venv/bin/pip install -e '.[dev]'
.venv/bin/python -m pytest
```

Optional extras, four: `.[tensor]` (TeNPy — the Layer 3 DMRG rung, the Layer 7 λφ⁴
chains and the operator-space TEBD behind the exact interacting QEI infimum), `.[sdp]`
(cvxpy — the general strong-local-passivity certificate, the smooth-entropy SDPs of the
resource theory and its free-instrument certificate), `.[diff]` (JAX — the Layer 6
differentiable stack: mirror, objectives, optimizers, multistart search, inverse design)
and `.[ibm]` (qiskit + qiskit-aer + qiskit-ibm-runtime — the Ikeda protocol on Aer, the
Layer 7 hardware simulator, and the L6 real-device on-ramp with its fake-backend
rehearsal). Every extra-gated feature has a core-only fallback — ED for DMRG, the closed
FFH certificate for the SDP, the plain-numpy stack plus CEM search and the numpy
distillation back end for JAX, dense linear algebra for every circuit — so the full
anchor suite runs on the default install: the extras are imported only inside their
guarded modules, `import vacuum` and every subpackage import with all four absent, and
the gated tests skip through `pytest.importorskip` (`tests/test_exports.py` pins both
facts, blocking tenpy, cvxpy, jax, jaxlib, qiskit, qiskit_aer and qiskit_ibm_runtime at
the meta path). Everything runs on a laptop; the λφ⁴ sweep is the one notebook that wants
half an hour.

## Repo map

The plan's tree lives as the Python package `vacuum/`, with `experiments/`, `papers/`,
`tests/`, and `docs/` at the repo root:

```
vacuum/
  core/          # covariance matrices, symplectic algebra, Williamson, Gaussian channels,
                 # harmonic-chain + Srednicki radial models, precision escalation ladder
  fermions/      # tight-binding correlation matrices, Peschel entanglement Hamiltonians
  audits/        # energy ledger, passivity, causality, precision — run on everything
  detectors/     # Layer 2: UDW perturbative + Gaussian nonperturbative harvesting engine,
                 # the circuit -> lattice coupling derivation, and the MI / discord /
                 # coherence measures of the Layer 2 pivot
  qet/           # Layer 3: quantum energy teleportation, strong-local-passivity maps
  inequalities/  # Layer 3: QEI stress-tester, sharp-constant search, work statistics
  modular/       # Layer 4: exact modular Hamiltonians of one, two and many intervals
                 # (Peschel–Eisler, Casini–Huerta dictionary, mpmath auto-escalation,
                 # the corner-transfer-matrix closed form for the gapped edge slope)
  geometry/      # Layer 4: ternary MERA, MI geometry + failure map, HaPPY code,
                 # toy-Jacobson residual (leap L2)
  floquet/       # Layer 5: Floquet engine, Mathieu chart, DCE anchor, lossy runs,
                 # the proved amplification bounds (Prüfer clock, Meissner rate optimum),
                 # pump-enhanced harvesting composite with its M2.5 split
  opt/           # Layer 6: JAX mirror (gjax), objectives + constrained optimizers,
                 # CEM search, BBPSSW/DEJMPS distillation, inverse design, the
                 # multistart global search and the nonperturbative signaling split
  composite/     # Layer 7 L4: harvest-then-teleport composite ledger, Fock conditioning
  interacting/   # Layer 7 L5: lambda-phi^4 chains via DMRG/TEBD, the interacting QEI —
                 # variational family and the exact operator-space-TEBD + DMRG infimum
  hardware/      # Layer 7 L6: qubit-encoded vacuum on Aer, VQE, protocols as circuits,
                 # sourced noise calibration, survival sweeps, and the audited
                 # real-device on-ramp (batch, submission, provenance record, analysis)
experiments/     # parameter files mirroring published setups (circuit QED, DCE, IBM QET)
scripts/         # one-command entry points (run_ibm_free_tier.py — the L6 device run)
papers/          # one directory per result candidate: notebook, data, draft, MANIFEST
tests/           # pytest suite — the literature as permanent regression tests
docs/API.md      # the coordination contract every module codes against
docs/STATUS.md   # per-layer ledger: verified / candidate / open
PLAN.md          # the research plan, Layers 0–7
```

Each package carries a README with that layer's build brief, anchors, discovery targets,
pivot trigger, and its API in the house style. The binding signatures are `docs/API.md`
and `docs/PLAN_LAYERS_2_3.md` for Layers 0–3 and each package's README for Layers 4–7;
the shipped surface is each package's `__all__`, and `tests/test_exports.py` pins that
every public name of every submodule is reachable from its package (the two JAX mirrors
`vacuum.opt.gjax` / `vacuum.opt.gjax_detectors` are reached as namespaces, since they
carry the same names as `vacuum.core` / `vacuum.detectors` on purpose). The resource
theory lives in `vacuum.qet.resource`, the 4d sharp-constant minimizer in
`vacuum.inequalities.qei`.

## The literature is the test suite

Every reproduced result from the anchor papers becomes a permanent regression test, and
each test records its measured number in the assertion message so the value is visible in
failures. New physics claims are admissible only from a build in which the entire
known-results suite passes — the field's history run as continuous integration. This is
the single most important discipline in the program: trust is manufactured in Layer 1 and
spent in Layers 2–7.

## Anomaly protocol (summary)

When something looks impossible, suspect the numerics first — precision escalation,
lattice-artifact checks, switching-function smoothness, the communication-vs-vacuum
split — then the model assumptions, and only a clean anomaly that survives the full audit
stack on two independent implementations enters the discovery register as a candidate.
The priors are explicit: passivity, the quantum inequalities, and causality are expected
to hold, and the fastest route to embarrassment is announcing their violation from a
rounding error at ν = 1/2.

The register has its first entry, and it went the way the priors say it should. On the
first pass of the UV-cutoff bracket the **energy-ledger closure audit fired** on one cell
of `circuit-qed-noise-thresholds` — lattice spacing a = 0.292 (N = 103), T = 80 mK,
Γ_φ = 1e9 Hz — with a defect of −1.0107e−9 against the *absolute* 1e−9 criterion: a 1 %
overshoot, and 4.45e−12 of that state's own energy (⟨H⟩ = 227.0, 16384 channel entries
plus 8192 work entries), i.e. float64 accumulation over the entry count rather than a
physics defect (the archive's `audit_event` keeps its producing run's superseded wording —
"⟨H⟩ = 230, 8192 booked channel entries" — which the MANIFEST corrects).
**The tolerance was not relaxed.** The single *synthetic* Γ_φ column above the device's
range (1e9 Hz) was dropped from the bracket surfaces instead; both device anchors (1e7
and 2e8 Hz) are retained, E_N is exactly 0 on the dropped column at every cutoff already
measured, and every row reported passed the unrelaxed audit (worst defect 4.73e−10). The
event and the action are carried in that candidate's MANIFEST and in
`data/cutoff_bracket.json → audit_event`. The diagnosis was then confirmed by a 41-run
scaling study and the criterion was the marginal element: the ledger closure criterion now
**scales** with the booked energies and operation count — tol = max(1e−12,
4ε·√n_modes·n_ops·scale), corrected — which at the two motivating cells happens to loosen it 102×, while it is ~1000× stricter for every protocol whose roundoff floor sits below 1e−9; the two
circuit-QED cells that motivated it, with what it cannot catch (a mis-booking small against
the protocol's own energy scale) stated in `docs/STATUS.md`, Layer 0; the tolerance itself
was never raised.
